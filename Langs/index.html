Interactive reading texts

Language Readers

Short texts for language learners, one page each: every word is coloured by its grammatical role and carries a tooltip with its dictionary form, a note and the English meaning. Click a word to look it up in online dictionaries; listen to the recording while you read.

How to use a reader
  • Hover over a word (or tap it on a phone) to see its reading, dictionary form and meaning.
  • Click a word to open it in the dictionaries you tick at the top of the page; each dictionary keeps its own tab.
  • Listen with the audio player above the text; the recording reads the whole text at a slightly slowed pace.
  • Pages work offline once loaded, follow the system dark mode, and need no plug-ins.
