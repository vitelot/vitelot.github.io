Cordata

Chess on a 5×8 board with kings, rooks, knights and pawns. Two or more pawns in a column form a battery, which advances as a block and captures straight ahead; a pawn that reaches the last rank wins, and so does the player who stalemates the opponent. Play a friend on the same board or play the engine: only legal moves are allowed, and the numbers in brackets point to the paragraphs of the rules.
